SUMIF & SUMIFS REFERENCE

Summing Values Matching Conditions

Concept & Syntax

Adds numbers inside a sum range that satisfy criteria conditions. SUMIFS handles multiple rules and places the sum range first.

=SUMIFS(sum_range, crit_range1, criteria1, ...)
Where is it Used

Consolidating department expenditure logs, summing regional sales metrics, and updating monthly KPI charts dynamically.

Example Model: Sales Sums
Dept (B) Sales (C)
Analytics120000
Finance85000
=SUMIFS(C2:C3, B2:B3, "Analytics") 120000
Common Mistakes Mismatched range sizes between sum and criteria arrays skews calculation offsets. Forgetting to lock ranges during formula drag.
Interview Prep Q: "What is the difference in range placement between SUMIF and SUMIFS?"
Expectation: Explain that SUMIF places the optional sum range last, whereas SUMIFS puts the sum range first. Point out that SUMIFS is preferred.
