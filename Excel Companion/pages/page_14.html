TODAY, DATE, & DATEDIF

Concept & Syntax

Excel stores dates as sequential integers (serial days since 1/1/1900). DATEDIF computes exact time intervals.

=DATEDIF(start, end, "Y"|"M"|"D")
Where is it Used

Tracking employee tenure milestones, age categorizations, and setting contract deadline warnings dashboards.

Example Model: Tenure Check
Join Date (B) Target (C)
15-07-202121-05-2026
=DATEDIF(B2, C2, "Y") 4 Years
Common Mistakes Date order error: placing the start date after the end date throws a `#NUM!` error. Hardcoding text dates.
Interview Prep Q: "Why do date columns sometimes display as numbers like 45000?"
Expectation: Explain that Excel stores dates as integers. A value of 45000 represents 45,000 days since 1900. Format to 'Date' to resolve.
