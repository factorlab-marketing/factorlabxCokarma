IF & IFS LOGICAL BRANCHING

Logical Testing & Segmentations

Concept & Syntax

Evaluates conditional tests and returns specified values. IFS checks multiple criteria in sequence and stops on the first True logic.

=IFS(t1, v1, t2, v2, ..., TRUE, default)
Where is it Used

Segmenting customers into priority tiers, defining transaction commission thresholds, and wrapping calculations in error handlers.

Example Model: Sales Tiers
Sales (B) Commission (C)
12000012000 (10%)
450000 (0%)
=IFS(B2>=100000, B2*10%, B2>=50000, B2*5%, TRUE, 0) 12000
Common Mistakes Out-of-order check limits inside IFS (placing lower thresholds before higher ones, e.g. B2>=50000 before B2>=100000 triggers wrong commissions).
Interview Prep Q: "How do you simplify nested IF statements?"
Expectation: Advise moving to IFS to keep formulas clean, or utilizing lookup tables with approximate matches for multi-tier criteria mappings.
