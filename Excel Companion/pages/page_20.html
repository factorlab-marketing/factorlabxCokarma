EOMONTH & NETWORKDAYS

Concept & Syntax

NETWORKDAYS computes working days excluding weekends/holidays. EOMONTH returns the last day of the month X months ahead.

=NETWORKDAYS(start, end, [holidays]) | =EOMONTH(start, m)
Where is it Used

Calculating SLA task business durations, setting billings cycles end-dates, and tracking monthly schedules.

Example Model: SLA Days
Start (B) End (C)
01-04-202530-04-2025
=NETWORKDAYS(B2, C2) 22 Days
Common Mistakes Forgetting to supply a holiday exclusion range inside NETWORKDAYS, which miscalculates true SLA business durations.
Interview Prep Q: "How do you get the first day of the current month dynamically?"
Expectation: Use EOMONTH to get the end of the previous month and add 1 day: =EOMONTH(TODAY(), -1) + 1.
