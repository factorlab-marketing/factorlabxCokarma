Traditional Instruments

Insurance & Fixed Deposits

Fixed Deposits

  • Concept: Lending money to a bank for a fixed tenure.
  • Primary Benefit: Capital protection and guaranteed (nominal) returns.
  • Returns: Fixed interest rates (Taxable).
  • Consideration: Post-tax returns may struggle to beat high inflation.

Traditional Insurance

  • Concept: Risk management tool for life/health coverage.
  • Investment Policies: Endowment/Moneyback policies combine insurance + savings.
  • Stability: Long-term commitments with surrender clauses.
  • Consideration: Insurance and Investment goals are often best kept separate for efficiency.
