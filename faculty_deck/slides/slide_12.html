How to Filter the Best Funds?

Critical parameters for analyzing Mutual Fund performance.

Alpha Generation

"Did it beat the average?"

Extra returns generated by the fund manager over the benchmark index. Positive Alpha = Good.

Volatility

"How bumpy is the ride?"

Measure of how much the price fluctuates. Lower volatility means a smoother investment journey.

Max Drawdown

"Worst case scenario"

The maximum fall from a peak to a trough before a new peak is achieved. Lower is better.

Risk-Adjusted Return

"Worth the risk?"

Returns calculated relative to the risk taken. High returns with high risk are less impressive.

Fund Age

"Track record"

Ideally >5 years. Shows how the fund handled different market cycles (bull and bear).

AUM & Liquidity

"Ease of exit"

Fund size matters. Too small implies risk; too large can limit agility. Liquidity ensures easy redemption.
