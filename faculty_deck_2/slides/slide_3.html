Three Paths of Wealth

Which investor profile defines your future?

Category A

Safe but Stagnant

Blended Return ~6% Nominal
Tax Impact 30% Slab (Heavy)
Inflation Beat Failed (-0.3% Real)
Verdict

Zero Real Wealth Growth

Real Life Example: Prof. Hari

Invested (20 Yrs)

₹10 Lakhs

Real Value Today

₹9.8 Lakhs ❌

The Problem: Although his nominal portfolio looks like ₹32L, inflation has eaten everything.

When his child's education costs rose to ₹15L, he had to borrow money because his corpus didn't grow enough to cover it.

"He prioritized guaranteed returns, but got guaranteed stagnation."

Category B

Illiquid & High Effort

Net Return ~7% (After Costs)
Liquidity ZERO (Months to sell)
Hassle Factor High (Tenants/Maint)
Verdict

Capital Locked & Modest Growth

Real Life Example: Prof. Meera

Invested (20 Yrs)

₹10 Lakhs

Real Value Today

₹11.5 Lakhs ⚠️

The Illusion: Property price doubled, but maintenance, vacancies, and transaction costs ate the profits.

When she needed emergency funds, she was stuck. Selling took 6 months. She missed the opportunity to grow it to ₹1 Cr in Equity.

"Rich on paper, poor in liquidity."

Category C

Wealth & Freedom

Blended Return ~12% Nominal
Real Growth 4x Growth (Post Tax)
Liquidity 100% Anytime
Verdict

Real Wealth + Peace

Real Life Example: Prof. Ravi

Invested (20 Yrs)

₹10 Lakhs

Real Value Today

₹42 Lakhs ✅

The Outcome: His SIPs compounded silently. When his child's marriage came up, the fund was ready.

For retirement, he has ₹1.4 Crores. He beat inflation, beat taxes, and never had to deal with tenants or lock-in periods.

"Money worked for him, while he slept."
