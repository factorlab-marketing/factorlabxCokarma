Mutual Funds

Professional Wealth Management

A Mutual Fund is a trust that collects money from a number of investors who share a common investment objective.

This money is invested in equities, bonds, money market instruments, and other securities. Each investor owns units, which represent a portion of the holdings of the fund.

Key mechanism: SIP

Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) allow you to invest small amounts periodically (e.g., monthly), aiding in rupee-cost averaging.

Professional Management

Managed by qualified Fund Managers.

Diversification

Spreads risk across different sectors and companies.

Liquidity

Open-ended funds allow redemption on demand.

Regulated

Strictly regulated by SEBI for investor protection.
